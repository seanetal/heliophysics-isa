Participants who only attend the 15-day lecture cycle will receive a training certificate. Meanwhile, those who also submit the two projects based on details provided by the ISA team within 30 days after the lectures end will receive a proof-of-internship certificate.

## Introduction and History

Heliophysics is the scientific study of the Sun's dynamic processes and their influence on the solar system, encompassing solar physics,
heliospheric physics, and space weather phenomena. The term "heliophysics" emerged in the late 20th century, formalized by NASA in
the 2000s to unify previously fragmented disciplines like solar physics, cosmic ray physics, ionospheric physics, and magnetospheric physics
into a cohesive framework.

Greek philosopher Hipparcus was one of the firsts to study the sun, and also make a scale to measure stars according to their luminosity. The scale ranged from 1 to 6, where 1 were the most brightest (like the sun) and 6 was stars that could only fainlty be seen with the naked eye. We still use a similar scale today to classify stars, only our scale is logarithmic in nature as opposed to Hipparcus' linear scale.
